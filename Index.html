About the GW Williams Neuroscience Club

Empowering Student Scientists

The GW Williams Neuroscience Club is a student-led initiative at Dr. G.W. Williams Secondary School. Designed for students enthusiastic about neuroscience, biology, medicine, psychology, and interdisciplinary research.

Our goal is to make complex scientific concepts accessible and exciting. Whether you aspire to attend medical school, pursue laboratory research, or simply want to understand how your brain processes memories and emotions, our community provides the mentorship and opportunities you need.

Student-Led

Driven by passion, peer mentorship, and collaborative curiosity.

Hands-On STEM

Interactive study tools, case studies, and lab simulations.

Competition Ready

Dedicated prep for Brain Bee, CBO, UTSRI, and CWSF.

Invasive Curiosity

Open to all students from Grade 9 to Grade 12.

Biology $25 Fee • April

Canadian Biology Olympiad (CBO)

Advanced nationwide biology exam. Winners qualify to represent Canada at the International Biology Olympiad. Requires teacher registration.

• Timing: Takes place in April

• Registration: $25 fee per student (Teacher registration required)

Fast-paced buzzer-based science competition covering broad topics across human biology, physics, neuroscience, chemistry, and history of science.
